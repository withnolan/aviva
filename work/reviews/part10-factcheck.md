## Must-fix wording changes (short list)

1. **#2** Replace "earliest known paper at Fangmatan" with "earliest surviving paper at Fangmatan" (paper abstract and §4), or "the earliest surviving paper bearing a drawing". Same change in changelog e1.n ("known" to "surviving"). Reason: Baqiao (2nd century BCE) is of uncertain date and status, and the sources say "extant".
2. **#6** Change "usually" to "often" in c3 ("its impression often stays") and in the paper §2 ("the impression of the writing often remains"). The forensic source says "sometimes" and "may remain".
3. **#15** Draw the mountain fold as dash-dot-dot (dash then two dots; dash-dot is also accepted), valley as plain dashes. Never a plain dotted line (that means a hidden edge).
4. **X1** If the UV moment is built: say "most white office paper" (not "all"), and keep the blue glow described as the brightener re-emitting UV as blue.
