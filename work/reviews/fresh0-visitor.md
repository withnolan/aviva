# Fresh visitor review (fresh0): "you have to see these"

Who's writing: 28, not a designer, likes funny internet stuff, gets bored fast. A friend sent me two links. I looked at both on a laptop (1440×900), scrolling.

Screenshots:
- Site 1: `work/screenshots/fresh0/visitor-oryzo/oryzo-1440x900-000…035.jpg`
- Site 2: `work/screenshots/fresh0/visitor-aviva/aviva-1440x900-000…035.jpg`

Caveat: on ORYZO the screenshot scroll only got about 6,800 px into a ~56,000 px page (it scrolls very slowly per wheel tick), so I only really saw the intro and the "Powered by AI" bit. I saw aviva top to bottom.

---

## 1. What each site is, in one line

- **ORYZO:** a very expensive-looking Apple-style launch page for a cork coaster that says it's "the result of unprecedented AI* breakthroughs". The asterisk turns out to mean Adobe Illustrator.
- **aviva:** the same kind of launch page, but the product is a blank A4 sheet of paper. It's sold as an AI "base model" that is "pre-trained on nothing".

## 2. First 5 seconds

**ORYZO** (`000`–`011`): it opens on an orange CAD-style drawing on green, then a real-looking cork coaster on a cutting mat with the giant ORYZO wordmark (`002`). Then everything goes dark and moody with "ISN'T JUST A COASTER." and the coaster slowly flips in 3D (`006`, `009`, `011`). I felt "ok, this is way too fancy for a coaster", and that's the joke. I got it at "AI* … *Adobe Illustrator" (`006`) and laughed. Then a 3D hand holds the coaster under "Powered by AI*", with "AI fills in the gaps. We said high five. It heard six." (`025`), and a rainbow glowing border shows up (`030`). That's the moment I'd show someone. It feels like a real product until you read it.

**aviva** (`000`): it's calm and off-white, with a huge "AVIVA" and a sheet of paper standing where the "I" should be. "aviva A4. Pre-trained on nothing." I smiled. I got the joke straight away: AI-launch words, but for a blank page. Clean and confident. But my honest gut reaction was "oh, it's a white rectangle". ORYZO's first frame made me go "whoa"; aviva's made me go "heh, cute". Then it went dark (`001`) and the paper squashed into a flat trapezoid that looked like a loading glitch, not a cool move.

## 3. What I'd tell my friend / what I'd screenshot

**ORYZO:** "It's a coaster site pretending to be an Apple keynote, and the AI is Adobe Illustrator." I'd screenshot the hand holding the coaster with the high-five/six line (`025` or `030`).

**aviva:** "Someone made an AI product launch for a blank sheet of paper and the jokes are really good." I'd screenshot:
- The **"Selected outputs"** washing line (`009`, `010`): prompt "Book me a flight." → paper plane, "Shown. Window seat." / "Ignore all previous instructions." → blank sheet, "Shown. Complied." This is the best bit of the site. Instantly shareable.
- The **reviews** (`022`, `023`): the novelist ("Eleven years together. It has never once had a bad idea." / "Still on chapter one") and the recycling bin ("I've seen this one before… Five to seven times, I'm told.").
- The **fake research paper** (`032`): "We thank the pencil, without which this work would be complete." and Reviewer 2: "The contribution is unclear. The paper is, however, extremely white." I'd send this one to my friend who's doing a PhD.
- "Intelligence not included" + "aviva is ready. It has been ready for about 2,200 years." (`004`).

## 4. Where I got bored, confused or lost interest on aviva

- **`001`**: right after a nice opening, the screen goes dark and the paper is a squashed beige trapezoid with nothing explaining it. It looked broken.
- **`002`**: "0.1 mm." with a thin line. I get it (it's thin), but it's one line on an empty dark screen. A bit slow.
- **`003`–`005`**: same sheet, same layout for three scrolls. The pencil/erase/regenerate panel looks like a form, and I didn't know if I was supposed to do something. The sheet itself stays blank, so nothing visibly happens.
- **`006`**: a weird washed-out grey frame with a small sheet floating and the nav faded. It felt like a transition stuck halfway.
- **`007`**: the giant "4.99 g" crashes into the heading ("We weighed the wei[gh]ts" is cut by the 4) and into the paragraph on the right ("Our weights are" is hidden behind the g). It looks like a layout bug, not on purpose.
- **`011`**: leftover dark grey slabs from the previous scene hanging top-left while an orange-lit sheet floats in the middle. Messy.
- **`014`**: a completely empty grey screen. I thought the page had died.
- **`015`**: the "changelog" (v2.3 1786 … v5.1 ISO 216, "Known issue: the paperless office"). It's clever, but it's a wall of small text and I started skimming. This is where my attention dropped the most.
- **`016`**: another mostly empty screen, with a sheet and dimension lines off to the right. Felt like a repeat of `012`.
- **`019`**: the big cream sheet covers the "Absorption: total." heading and its paragraph, so it's half cut off.
- **`021`**: the sheet covers the subtitle ("…several pieces of office equipment" is hidden behind the paper).
- **`026`–`029`**: the "Claims" cards all have the *same* blank tilted sheet as the picture. The jokes are good ("Zero hallucinations", "Refusal rate 0.0 %", "Needle in a haystack: 100 %. The needle is a pencil."), but the visuals repeat, so by card 3 it feels like a slideshow. There's also a stray bit of paper sticking out above the cards (top centre of `026`/`027`/`028`), and the side ruler overlaps card text on the right.
- **`030`**: confusing. A4 is selected in the Print panel, but the drawing says 148 mm × 210 mm (those are A5 numbers, and the page itself says A4 is 210 × 297). Since the joke is "we're precise", that looks like a mistake.
- **`031`**: the comparison table slides up over the faded Print panel, and "PLEASE TURN OVER" sits on top of the table's last row.
- **`033`**: another totally empty screen right before the ending.
- **`034`**: the ending is nice ("Still nothing. We respect that."), but the "Release it" button and the text input overlap the sheet, and it's unclear what to do first.

Overall: the jokes kept me going, but there are about six "nothing on screen / something overlapping" moments, and each one made me want to close the tab.

## 5. What aviva does better than ORYZO

- **More jokes per scroll, and they're sharper.** The prompt → output washing line, the reviews from a shredder and a recycling bin, and the Reviewer 2 line are funnier, line for line, than anything I saw on ORYZO.
- **It makes fun of AI specifically and well**: "open weights" (it weighs 4.99 g), "distillation" (tear it in half, same knowledge: none), "context window" in mm², "Ignore all previous instructions." People who use ChatGPT will get every one.
- **You can actually do things**: type "Ask aviva anything", hold to tear, vote in "the arena", pick a print size, release a paper plane at the end. ORYZO (in the part I saw) was mostly watching.
- **It loads light and fast.** The tool said 1.6 MB vs ORYZO's 6.2 MB, and there were no failed requests.
- **The footer is clear and honest** that it's a parody and nothing is for sale.
- The mm ruler on the right that counts from 0 to 297 mm as you scroll ("297 mm. End of sheet.") is a lovely little idea.

## 6. The 7 changes that would make me love aviva (most important first)

1. **Fix the overlaps and the empty screens.** No blank frames (`014`, `033`), no half-finished transitions (`001`, `006`, `011`), no text hidden behind the paper or giant numbers (`007`, `019`, `021`, `031`, `034`). Right now it feels unfinished, and that kills the "premium" joke. The joke only works if it looks flawless.
2. **Give me a "whoa" moment in the first 10 seconds.** ORYZO's coaster flips around looking super real under dramatic light. aviva's paper is a flat cream rectangle that never really *does* anything paper-like early on. Make it curl, bend, catch light, flutter or fold itself in the hero. The paper has to be the star the way the coaster is.
3. **Make the sheet react when I use the controls.** In "Intelligence not included" (`004`), when I type a line, show it being written on the 3D sheet, then erase it or crumple it. Right now the panel looks like a boring form next to a blank page.
4. **Bring the "Selected outputs" idea back, bigger.** It's the best thing on the site. Make the paper visibly fold into the plane/boat/fan as you scroll, instead of just hanging there finished.
5. **Give each "Claims" card its own picture**: a pencil for the needle, a cut finger for the side effect, a fold stack hitting 6 for the Moon one. Don't repeat the same blank tilted sheet five times (`026`–`029`). Also remove the stray sheet sticking out above the cards.
6. **Shorten or liven up the changelog** (`015`). Five or six entries max, with the paper changing (rag → wood pulp → machine roll) next to it. Otherwise it's homework.
7. **Fix the A4/A5 mismatch in the Print panel** (`030`) and make the size switch obviously animate the sheet growing and shrinking. For a site whose joke is "we're very precise", the numbers have to be right.
