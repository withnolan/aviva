## Must-fix wordings

1. **#9** `s06b.call.2`: "FOLDS 1–2: CORNERS TO THE CENTRE LINE, 45°" becomes **"FOLD 1: CORNERS TO THE CENTRE LINE, 45°"**, or "FOLDS 1–2: EDGES TO THE CENTRE LINE. NOSE: 45°". Fold 2's crease is at 22.5°, not 45°.
2. **#11** `s08.c3.cite`: "European Paper Recycling Council, 2024" becomes **"European Paper Recycling Council, 2025"** (76.4 %, which also revises 2024 to 76.4 %). In `s08.c2`, the label "RECYCLED IN EUROPE" becomes **"RECYCLING RATE, EUROPE"**, because the rate includes exported paper for recycling.
3. **#10** `s08.c1`: **"Usually five to seven times. Researchers are aiming for 25."** (restores the checked hedge).
4. **#4** `s04.weights.sr`: **"An open case of eight calibration weights that add up to 4.99 grams."** (the leaf weights are nickel silver, not brass). The props stay two brass knobs and six silver-coloured leaves.
