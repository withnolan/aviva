MATERIALS

No glue. Just attraction.

Up close, aviva is a mat of cellulose fibres. As it dries, hydrogen bonds between neighbouring fibres hold it together. Nobody taught it that.

Fibres: millimetres long, a fraction of a hair wide.

AFTERLIFE

It comes back.

aviva starts as wood pulp. After use, its fibres can be made into paper again, and again, and again. Then they retire.

  • 5–7

    LIVES

    Paper fibres are usually said to survive five to seven trips through recycling, each one leaving them shorter and stiffer. Researchers are aiming for 25.

  • ≈ ¾

    RECYCLED IN EUROPE

    About three-quarters of the paper and board used in Europe is recycled: 75.1 % in 2024, according to the European Paper Recycling Council.

  • ≈ 4.6 g

    CO₂e PER SHEET (ISO METHOD)

    One study of an A4 sheet of office paper found 4.3–4.7 g of CO₂e, depending on the method: 4.6 g by ISO’s. The sheet itself weighs about 5 g.

    Dias & Arroja, Journal of Cleaner Production, 2012.
