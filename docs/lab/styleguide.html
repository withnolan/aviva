01

Colour

Paper white, graphite and one ink. Version 2 adds a cool charcoal, cool greys and three stationery colours: pencil yellow, blueprint blue and a highlighter. One accent family per screen; the sheet is always the brightest, warmest white.

DECISION #24 · ADDED

Contrast, every ground

WCAG 2.x · COMPUTED LIVE FROM THE ROLE VARIABLES
GroundTextMutedAccentDimsFocus ringUI boundaryPrimary buttonUnder a mark

Text and labels need 4.5 : 1 at any size; focus rings, UI boundaries and the pencil accent (a non-text mark) need 3 : 1. Alpha colours are composited over their ground; the mark is the highlighter (mask alpha 0.88) multiplied over light grounds, or lying under graphite text on charcoal. The same table, with every pair the site uses, is printed by node work/scripts/design/contrast-table.mjs.

02

Grounds

The same markup on every ground. Components only use role variables, so data-ground recolours them: paper (default), light grey, cool grey, charcoal and ink.

03

Type

Hanken Grotesk, variable 300–500, self-hosted (30 KB). Light for display and headings, Regular for body, Medium for labels. Nothing is bold. Figures are tabular by default. DM Mono appears only in the BibTeX box. Sizes are fluid from 390 to 1440 px; the readouts show this viewport.

Space, shape and motion

Measured gutters, square corners (2 px at most), solid hairlines, crop marks and dog-ears. Two soft shadows. Motion arrives and settles; anything tied to scroll is scrubbed, never timed.

9.9 · SPEC SHEET-SHAPED, INK SURFACE (REQUEST 15.3)

  • 5–7

    LIVES

    Paper fibres are usually said to survive five to seven trips through recycling, each one leaving them shorter and stiffer. Researchers are aiming for 25.

  • ≈ ¾

    RECYCLED IN EUROPE

    About three-quarters of the paper and board used in Europe is recycled: 75.1 % in 2024, according to the European Paper Recycling Council.

  • ≈ 4.6 g

    CO₂e PER SHEET (ISO METHOD)

    One study of an A4 sheet of office paper found 4.3–4.7 g of CO₂e, depending on the method: 4.6 g by ISO’s. The sheet itself weighs about 5 g.

    Dias & Arroja, Journal of Cleaner Production, 2012.
